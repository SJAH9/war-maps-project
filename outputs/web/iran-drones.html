Reading this network

This is a limited current-conflict report layer, not an all-time inventory. Missile-only reports are excluded. Mixed-weapon reports are off by default. Interceptions, debris, reported targeting and impacts remain identified in each source record; they are not all counted as successful strikes. A country is the source's incident location, not necessarily the nationality of a ship, victim or target.

Source: Iran Attacks Map incident register, CC BY 4.0. Source methodology. The source is a public-report aggregator, separate from War Maps' UCDP network. A reproducible text screen selects explicit Iran/IRGC drone mentions and excludes named proxy and suspected records; it can omit relevant incidents and does not independently establish launch responsibility. Open the source articles to assess attribution, disputed claims and outcomes.
