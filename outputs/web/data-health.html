Mortality, fertility, and birth rate

IHME and World Bank / UN observations

Population-health layers combine Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation Global Burden of Disease 2023 results with the World Bank's WDI crude-birth-rate series, sourced primarily from UN World Population Prospects.

Two birth measures

Total fertility and crude birth rate are not interchangeable. Total fertility estimates births per woman across reproductive ages. Crude birth rate divides annual live births by the entire mid-year population. The interface keeps their controls, units, colors, and normalization separate.

Reading health with conflict

The world explorer can draw an IHME health choropleth beneath conflict events while retaining the selected conflict window. The Life and Death scene compares conflict fatalities, total population, mortality, fertility, and crude birth rate using separate stacks; the inspector always reports native values and units.

Reading health with government forms

When a regime category is selected, matching country-year locations are outlined over the health field. The related-data panel reports descriptive group means and transitions supported by overlapping V-Dem and IHME years. Unequal coverage, confounding, lag, and measurement differences remain visible limitations.
