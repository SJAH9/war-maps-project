Sources and boundaries

hormuz.now open data supplies positions, crossing counts, the counting gate and sampled tracks under CC BY 4.0. Snapshot refresh is approximately 20 seconds; crossing totals refresh approximately once per minute. Cached data remains visible on failure, with its age disclosed. No position is extrapolated.

IMF PortWatch provides the separately identified historical daily series. AIS gaps, spoofing and GPS interference can affect coverage. Tracks connect sampled reports and are not proof of the route taken between them. Country outlines use Natural Earth 1:10m reference geometry, not navigational charts.
