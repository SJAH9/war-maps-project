Data provenance for every map layer

Cite the atlas for project transformations and each upstream producer for the observations reused in analysis.

Uppsala Conflict Data Program

UCDP/PRIO Armed Conflict Dataset and UCDP Candidate Events Dataset. Cite the exact version disclosed in the repository build and follow UCDP terms.

V-Dem Institute

Varieties of Democracy country-year dataset. Cite the loaded release and the V-Dem codebook for regime and indicator definitions.

V-Dem dataset and documentation

IHME

Global Burden of Disease 2023 mortality and fertility results. Cite the GBD 2023 study and comply with IHME download terms.

World Bank and UN Population Division

WDI indicators SP.DYN.CBRT.IN and SP.POP.TOTL distribute crude birth rate and total population, sourced primarily from UN World Population Prospects, under CC BY 4.0.
