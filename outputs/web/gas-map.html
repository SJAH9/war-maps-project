Read the boundary

Gasoline and diesel can be shown separately or together. Warm colors identify gasoline and blue colors identify diesel. Height remains strictly proportional to price; footprint supplies additional fixed global contrast, making low-price towers narrow and high-price towers broad without recalculating the range for a selected country.

How to compare

All displayed prices use one fixed global linear height scale from $0 to a $10-per-U.S.-gallon ceiling. A $5 tower is exactly half the height of a $10 tower in every filter and geography. Tower width uses one disclosed global cubic emphasis over that same scale, not a local minimum and maximum. Observations above $10 meet the ceiling while their exact price remains inspectable.
