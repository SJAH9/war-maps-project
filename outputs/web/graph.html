How to navigate

Pan: drag empty space. Rotate: hold Shift or Alt while dragging empty space. Zoom: scroll or use the fit button.

Move a node: drag it; release to give it momentum. Connected nodes follow gradually. Optimize: lays the selected network out in 3D, with connected nodes grouped around hubs.

Selection: selecting a nation narrows the view to its recorded conflict and organization relationships. Search and organization checkboxes focus the camera automatically. “Include ungrouped nations” restores nations outside the selected groups.

Evidence: observed connections are source-derived. Synthetic topology choices are visual comparison models, not evidence of relationships.
