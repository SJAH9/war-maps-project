NewsMedia

NewsBoob places international live-news channels in one tuner so differences in selection, timing, and framing can be compared. Broadcasts are context, not verified War Maps evidence, and a channel’s presence is not an endorsement.

Source boundary

The broadcasters control the streams.

NewsBoob supplies the interface and requests the selected publisher’s HLS stream. Availability, captions, program content, and transmission quality remain properties of each broadcaster or delivery network.

Privacy boundary

No War Maps analytics.

This project adds no analytics, advertising profile, account, or tracking cookie. Playing a channel makes network requests to that broadcaster and its content-delivery providers, which operate under their own policies.

Evidence boundary

Coverage is not confirmation.

Use live reporting to identify questions and compare public narratives. Use the linked source records and methods elsewhere in War Maps to establish what the project’s datasets actually support.
