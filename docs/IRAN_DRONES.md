# Iranian drone report network

Entry: `outputs/web/iran-drones.html`. Iran → reported incident country → original target/affected-place category. Selecting either level opens the dated incident register and its retained primary and backup links. An accessible country/category list follows the graphic. Date filters apply to all counts and registers.

Source: [Iran Attacks Map structured incident register](https://iranattacks.com/data), CC BY 4.0. This is an independent public-report aggregator, not UCDP and not original War Maps reporting. The generated snapshot retains source confidence, original summaries, categories, attack types, attribution wording and URLs. Coordinate data is retained in the downloadable source records but not plotted as operational impact points.

`python3 -m src.generate_iran_drones` downloads the source, records SHA-256 and UTC retrieval time, and applies a disclosed conservative text screen: drone in attack type or title, explicit Iran/Iranian/IRGC mention in title or summary, excluding named Hezbollah/Houthi/militia, Iran-backed/aligned, and suspected records. This is not a validated attribution classifier. It deliberately produces an incomplete current-conflict subset rather than claiming a global or historical census. Source claims can remain contested, including cases attributed by a government and denied by Iran. The source articles and original wording remain essential.

Mixed missile/rocket/drone attack types are a separate optional layer, off by default. Drone interceptions and debris damage are retained as such in the register; node and line counts are incident reports, never the number of weapons, successful hits, deaths, unique sites, or proven Iranian launches. Original target category can represent an affected place rather than an intended target. Country is the source incident-location field, including maritime assignments, not vessel nationality.

Initial snapshot: 105 selected records, 54 drone-specific records; nine countries. Data range is March–August 2026 and does not establish absence elsewhere. No automatic update cadence is asserted. Rebuild explicitly to refresh, then regenerate the atlas. Build does not alter the UCDP network.
