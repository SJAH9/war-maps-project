# Nested causal enclosure method

The atlas stores an observation before it stores a conclusion. Each observation has a source enclosure, time enclosure, geographic enclosure, actor enclosure, and coding-status enclosure. Conclusions are links across those records, not replacements for them.

## Ternary function

The basic form is:

```text
E = [E_outer | D_active | E_inner]
```

- `E_outer` is another enclosure function opening toward source conditions;
- `D_active` is the specified, addressable departure, relation, or observation; and
- `E_inner` is another enclosure function opening toward consequences and the information consumer.

This is not a positive, unknown, and negative classification table. The first and third positions always contain enclosure functions. The middle carries a departure identifier, its repeatable measurements, and the keys by which it can join further observations. The finite representation uses addressed `E` frontiers for depths not yet opened; a frontier is an enclosure waiting for the next question.

At a finite cut, the Final Frontier is expressed as the next unanswered question. When another observation becomes available, the frontier is replaced by a more detailed enclosure while the previous address remains meaningful. Propaganda remains enclosed as propaganda, with its producer, strategic setting, embedded claims, methods of identification, and unanswered questions intact.

## Prompt-directed causal projection

The data field is intentionally limited to Uppsala conflict observations and V-Dem state conditions. A user prompt chooses the active question. The project then opens the relevant outer conditions, local decisions, and enclosed consequences recursively. Initiation, motive, decision makers, war economies, trade relations, equipment, and information operations are not prohibited conclusions; they are discoveries to be reached through a documented causal path.

The projection must show its work:

```text
[ observed outer conditions | prompted question and causal departure | enclosed decisions, relations, and consequences ]
```

Each discovered relation can become the specified departure of another enclosure. The project therefore does not require a separate source-purpose-target media taxonomy to become resilient to propaganda. Omnirelevance follows from correct nesting: following outer enclosures reaches source conditions, while following inner enclosures reaches effects and information consumers. Purpose, decision makers, and hidden volatile relations appear when they become causally relevant at the addressed depth.

## Volatile findings

The project does not avoid volatile findings. Its discipline is to preserve the chain by which they become visible. A claim about who initiated a war, why a decision was taken, who profited, or which outer condition projected inward must remain connected to the observations, intermediate relations, methods, and frontier questions that produced it. This lets the atlas drive a conflict toward its documented causal head while keeping its sources and transformations inspectable.
