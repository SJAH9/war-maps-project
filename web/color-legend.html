The War Maps palette

A consistent semantic system across every view

Color identifies roles and measurement families. It does not label a side as morally good, legitimate, allied with the viewer, or responsible for a conflict.

SlateSide A and same-side relationships
OliveSide A depth, electoral or democratic context
OxbloodSide B and opposing relationships
ClaySide B depth and elevated conflict activity
ManilaBasemap, low values, and documentary surfaces
KhakiReference geography and neutral comparisons

Two-sided records

Within a selected conflict, Side A is always slate/olive and Side B is always oxblood/clay. These colors follow source-side positions and remain stable in maps, legends, networks, and nation relationships. They do not encode nationality or a permanent bloc.

Accessibility

Every semantic color is paired with text, shape, position, pattern, or an inspectable value. The system deliberately avoids red-white-blue combinations and does not rely on hue alone to communicate source status.
