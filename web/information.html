Project guide

How to read, verify, and reuse War Maps

War Maps joins conflict, governance, population-health, fuel-price, and civilian-casualty observations without hiding their different units, dates, or evidentiary boundaries. Use this guide to verify every displayed layer, understand the decisions that connect them, and learn what the interface cannot establish.
