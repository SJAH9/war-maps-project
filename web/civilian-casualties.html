Towers show UCDP GED deaths_civilians totaled by event country: civilians killed in state-based, non-state, and one-sided organized violence events. Combatant deaths are excluded. Indirect deaths (famine, disease, infrastructure collapse) are not in this field. Location is where UCDP coded the event, not the perpetrator's home state.

How to compare

Height and color use one linear scale across all mapped countries. GED 25.1 covers 1989–2024. Candidate events add 2026 through 31 July. 2025 is not in these releases. Missing countries are not confirmed zeroes. Rwanda's 1994 total dominates the world field; that is in the source, not a display exaggeration.
