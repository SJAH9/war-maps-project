Source guide

Gas and civilian-casualty map data

The Gas Map and Civilian Casualties map are source-dated views with different measurements. Fuel prices are a one-date market snapshot. Civilian casualties are event records selected by an inclusive year range. Neither layer is a live feed or a complete census.

Every price is converted for display to indicative U.S. dollars per U.S. gallon. Tower height uses one fixed global linear scale from $0 to a $10-per-gallon ceiling: $5 is exactly half the height of $10 in every region and fuel selection. Values above $10 meet the ceiling without changing the scale, while their exact values remain in the inspector and payload. The payload also retains original currency and unit. Most exchange rates use the European Central Bank reference rates; Taiwan uses the Central Bank of the Republic of China rate.

Civilian Casualties sources

The primary layer uses UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset 26.1 deaths_civilians values for 1989–2025 and the loaded UCDP candidate-event releases through August 2026. Values are summed by the country where UCDP locates the event and only within the selected inclusive year range. The default range is the latest ten calendar years, currently 2017–2026. Independent toggles place IHME GBD 2023 all-cause mortality and long-run life expectancy beside casualties. The latter cites Riley's peer-reviewed historical estimates, HMD, and UN WPP 2024. Health layers use only actual observations inside the selected interval and never extrapolate them.

Gaza and West Bank context records from the oPt Health Cluster / WHO Unified Health Dashboard remain outside UCDP and are never added to its world total. The Gaza dashboard observation replaces the overlapping older OCHA/MoH snapshot, whose provenance remains attached. AOAV's separate explosive-violence record is retained. These sources have different measurement boundaries and cannot be added or treated as equivalent civilian-only estimates. Injury counts are shown separately from deaths.

Download the frozen dashboard aggregates and injury-aware graph data: casualty reporting sequences, demographic tables and health-attack location links. Graph nodes retain deaths and injuries separately. Health-attack losses overlap area totals; cumulative daily rows must never be summed. The period-share demographic table contains fractions, not counts. No patient, contact or mailing-list records are ingested. Source URL, dates, query provenance and publisher terms accompany the data.

Reference geometry

Natural Earth supplies country outlines. U.S. state and Japanese prefecture reference geometry and GeoNames location coordinates support subnational placement. Geometry is a display reference and does not adjudicate borders.
