Support the work.

The War Maps Project is an independent public-interest research tool. If it is useful to you, you can support its continued development with bitcoin.

Support is welcome, never obligatory. This project was produced through the time and energy of its principal investigator and over a billion tokens. It satisfied the gnawing need to do something about the state of the world without taking a side or making futile sacrifices. It feels good to work on; it would not feel bad to contribute to it.
