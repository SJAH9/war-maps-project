Read the boundary

Gasoline and diesel can be shown separately or together. Warm colors identify gasoline and blue colors identify diesel. Striped magenta caps separate included taxes when a full breakdown is available. EU taxes are the difference between same-date inclusive and exclusive published prices; Australian taxes are calculated from the report-confirmed excise plus GST. Other observations remain unsplit because their sources do not supply a comparable full tax amount. Price dates remain individual to each source.

How to compare

All displayed prices use one fixed global linear height scale from $0 to a $10-per-U.S.-gallon ceiling. A $5 tower is exactly half the height of a $10 tower in every filter and geography. Tower width uses one disclosed global cubic emphasis over that same scale, not a local minimum and maximum. Observations above $10 meet the ceiling while their exact price remains inspectable. Tax caps above the ceiling are clipped, not rescaled; the inspector's amounts and composition bars show the complete price.
